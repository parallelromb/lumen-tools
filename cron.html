      Cron Format Guide (5-field standard)
    
    
    
    
      
    FieldValuesSpecialsExample
    
      
        
    Minute0-59* , - /*/5 = every 5 min
    
        
    Hour0-23* , - /9-17 = 9 AM to 5 PM
    
        
    Day of Month1-31* , - /1,15 = 1st & 15th
    
        
    Month1-12* , - /*/3 = every quarter
    
        
    Day of Week0-6 (Sun=0)* , - /1-5 = weekdays
    
          
    
    
    
    
      * = any value
      , = list (1,3,5)
      - = range (1-5)
      / = step (*/5)
